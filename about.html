Our Story

Turning Journeys into Stories

Mbeks Travels was born from a simple love for travel: the excitement of a new road, the quiet of a sunrise over the savanna, and the laughter of friends sharing a meal somewhere new. We started Mbeks Travels to share that feeling with you.

Every journey has a story. We believe travel is about more than reaching a destination. It's about the experiences, discoveries and memories made along the way. That's why we plan every trip around you: your interests, your pace and your budget.

We love showing travellers the best of home, from game drives in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth National Parks to the Nile in Jinja and the crater lakes of Fort Portal. When you're ready to go further, we'll help you get there, from the beaches of Kenya and Zanzibar to Rwanda, Dubai and the Maldives.

Whether it's your first safari, a beach escape, a weekend staycation or a group adventure, we're here to turn your journey into a story worth telling.
