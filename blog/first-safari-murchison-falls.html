Your First Safari in Murchison Falls: What to Expect

Murchison Falls is Uganda's largest national park and one of the best places for a first safari. Here is what a typical trip looks like, and how to get the most from it.

Getting there

The park sits in north-western Uganda. The road trip from Kampala takes most of a day, so many travellers break the journey or plan at least two nights in the park. We'll help you choose the route and stay that suits your group.

Game drives

Early mornings and late afternoons are the best times to see animals. Look out for elephants, giraffes, buffalo, antelope and, with a little luck, lions. A good guide makes all the difference, so ask plenty of questions.

On the Nile

A boat trip towards the bottom of the falls is a highlight, with hippos, crocodiles and birdlife along the banks. At the top of the falls, you can watch the Nile squeeze through a narrow gorge with incredible force.

What to pack

  • Neutral-coloured, comfortable clothes
  • A warm layer for chilly early-morning drives
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Insect repellent
  • Binoculars and a camera with spare batteries

When to go

The drier months are generally easier for game viewing, but the park is beautiful all year round. Tell us when you're free and we'll suggest the best plan for those dates.
