Weekend Staycations to Recharge Close to Home

You don't need a long flight to feel like you've had a real break. A weekend staycation gives you a change of scenery, time to rest and new memories, without the long journey.

Why a staycation works

Less travel time means more time to relax. Staycations are easier to plan, easier on your budget and perfect when you can only get away for two or three days.

Ideas for your next weekend

  • Jinja: riverside stays and adventure on the Nile
  • Fort Portal: crater lakes, tea plantations and cool green hills
  • Lakeside resorts: quiet days by the water, closer than you think

Make the most of it

  • Switch off: put your phone away and plan very little
  • Book ahead for public holidays and long weekends
  • Bring someone you love, or go solo and enjoy the quiet

Tell us when you're free and what kind of break you need, and we'll suggest a staycation that fits.
