Zanzibar on a Budget: Planning Your Beach Getaway

Turquoise water, white sand and history around every corner: Zanzibar feels like a dream trip, but it doesn't have to stretch your budget. Here's how to plan a beach getaway that gives you more for less.

Be flexible with your dates

Travelling outside the busiest holiday periods often means better value on flights and stays. If your dates are flexible, tell us, and we'll look for the best options.

Choose the right base

Stone Town is great for culture, food and history, while the beaches along the coast are perfect for relaxing. Splitting your stay between the two gives you the best of both.

Eat like a local

Some of the best food is the simplest. Try the evening food stalls at Forodhani Gardens and small local restaurants for fresh seafood and Swahili dishes.

Mix free and paid activities

  • Spend lazy days on the beach and swimming in the ocean
  • Wander the narrow streets of Stone Town
  • Add one or two special experiences, like a spice tour or a boat trip

Let us bundle it

Planning flights, transfers and stays together keeps things simple. Tell us your budget and what you love, and we'll shape a Zanzibar trip around it.
