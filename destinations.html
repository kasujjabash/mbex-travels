Uganda

The Pearl of Africa is where our story starts. Go on game drives across the savanna, watch the Nile thunder through rock, and wake up to misty hills and crater lakes. These are the places we love to show travellers first.

Ask about Uganda

Featured places

  • Murchison Falls National Park

    Uganda's largest national park, where the Nile forces its way through a narrow gorge. Game drives and river trips through classic savanna.

  • Queen Elizabeth National Park

    Open plains, the Kazinga Channel and a chance to spot the famous tree-climbing lions of Ishasha.

  • Jinja

    The source of the Nile and Uganda's adventure capital, with river activities and relaxed riverside stays.

  • Fort Portal

    Crater lakes, tea plantations and cool green hills, perfect for a scenic getaway or staycation.

  • And more across Uganda

    Have somewhere else in mind? Tell us where, and we'll help you plan it.

East Africa

Kenya

City energy in Nairobi and long, white beaches along the coast. Combine a city stay with ocean days in Mombasa, Diani or Malindi.

Ask about Kenya

Featured places

  • Nairobi

    Kenya's lively capital, with a national park right on the city's edge.

  • Mombasa

    A historic coastal city with an Old Town, warm water and beach resorts.

  • Diani Beach

    Powder-white sand and turquoise water on Kenya's south coast.

  • Malindi

    A relaxed north-coast town with marine parks and quiet beaches.
